**Negative control.** The last case is expected to be *accepted*. It documents finding C1: the Groth16 circuit binds the batch digest but does not verify the ML-DSA signature, so anyone with the public proving key can authorise a registered account's op without its signature. Today the guarantee that signatures are valid rests on the bundler's off-chain check (all `bundler` rows above). The case must flip to *rejected* once ML-DSA verification is proven inside a zkVM (docs/future-work-zkvm-linux.md).

Further on-chain cases (G2 point outside the subgroup, public input ≥ r, failed-batch rollback) are covered by `crates/evm/tests/wallet.rs`.
